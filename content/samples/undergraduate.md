## From Section 1: the problem in brief

> The problem, then, is that mobile money creates the potential for records, but it is not known whether traders turn that potential into practice. A 0.5% levy on withdrawals, introduced through the Excise Duty (Amendment) Act 2018 (as described in "The impact of mobile money taxation in Uganda," 2021), may push some traders back to cash and so erase the digital trail. I found no academic study of this question among traders in Mbale Central Market, where only news and government sources describe trading conditions.
